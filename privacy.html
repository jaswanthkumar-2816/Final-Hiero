Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Hiero helps you improve your resume and practise interviews. To do that we handle things that are personal to you: your CV, your profile links, and what you say during a mock interview.

This page explains exactly what we collect, who else sees it, and how to get it deleted. If something here is unclear, email us and we will answer in plain language.

03Mock interviews: your camera, microphone and recordings#

This section matters more than the rest, so we are being specific about it.

What is captured

A full mock interview uses both your camera and microphone. A practice session uses the microphone only, and the screen tells you which one you are starting before it begins.

What is stored

  • A video recording of the session, saved on our server so you can play the interview back.
  • An audio recording of each answer, saved alongside it.
  • A transcript of what you said, together with the questions, your scores and the written report.

Only you can access your recordings. They are not served publicly: each request is checked against your account first, and a recording cannot be reached by guessing a link.

How long they last

Recordings are stored on the server that runs Hiero, and that storage is not permanent — recordings are deleted whenever Hiero is updated or restarted, which happens regularly. Treat a recording as temporary: if you want to keep one, download it. Your transcript, scores and report are stored separately and are not affected.

Keep this honest as the code changes. This paragraph is true because uploads/recordings/ sits on an ephemeral filesystem with no persistent disk. The moment a disk is mounted or storage moves to S3, recordings start surviving and this paragraph becomes false — replace it with a real retention period and a deletion routine at the same time, not afterwards.

Your choices

You can delete any interview session and its recordings at any time, or ask us to delete all of them (section 7). We do not use your recordings or transcripts to train our own models, and we never share them with an employer unless you apply to an opportunity and choose to share your results.

If you would rather not be recorded, do not start a mock interview — the rest of Hiero works without it.

05Payments#

Paid plans are processed by Razorpay Software Private Limited. You enter your card, UPI or bank details on Razorpay's own checkout. Those details never reach Hiero's servers and we never store them.

We keep only a record of which plan you bought, when, and whether the payment succeeded, which we need for your account and for tax and accounting records.

07Your rights#

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Know and access what personal data of yours we hold and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct anything inaccurate, and complete anything missing. Most of this you can do yourself from your profile.
  • Erase your personal data, where we no longer need it for the purpose you gave it for or to meet a legal obligation.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawing consent for interview processing means you can no longer use mock interviews, but your account stays.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity.
  • Complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve it.

To exercise any of these, email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address on your account. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify who you are first — that protects you, not us.

08How we protect it#

  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes, never in a readable form.
  • Traffic to hiero.in is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.
  • Access to the production database and to our providers' dashboards is limited to the people who operate Hiero.
  • Our API keys and credentials are held in server configuration, not in our published source code.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

09Age#

Hiero is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10Cookies and local storage#

We use browser storage for things the product cannot work without: keeping you signed in, remembering your session, and saving drafts of resumes you are working on. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

11Changes to this policy#
