The pattern across all 20: nobody can prove what a candidate can actually do — so the market sells substitutes for evidence. Better wording (₹40,000 résumés), hollow certificates (₹5–13k unproctored tests), and now live cheating ($300/yr, undetectable). Money flows to faking it because proving it isn't possible.
Hiero's position: every other product helps you look ready. Hiero establishes whether you are — a proctored session, graded and re-tested, producing evidence a hiring manager can open and judge in ninety seconds.
