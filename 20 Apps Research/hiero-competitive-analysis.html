The pattern

Gaps that repeat across every platform

A gap in one product is a bug. A gap in eight is a market.
8/9

No verifiable proof of competence

Coursera certifies attendance. Udemy certifies watching. HackerRank's own docs disclaim any hiring endorsement. Unstop hands everyone a participation PDF. LinkedIn is retiring Skill Assessments because hirers want evidence of applied skill.

Hiero's answer: an assessment you can fail and can't fake, producing a record an employer can check.
7/9

No gap analysis against the target role

Nobody tells you the distance between who you are and the job you want. CV Sync is the only attempt — and it compares two text files, because the résumé is the only artifact it has.

Hiero's answer: JD in, capability measured, missing pieces named.
5/9

Nobody answers, nobody corrects your work

Content is delivered, scores are issued, and no one ever responds to what you actually did. Udemy has no instructors answering, HackerRank returns "wrong answer", Coursera's forums are dead.

Hiero's answer: feedback is the product; content is the commodity.
5/9

Diagnosis without remedy — the loop never closes

You're told you're weak at system design and left there. Internshala's remedy is a six-week course; Remasto's is nothing at all.

Hiero's answer: ask → score → teach → re-test, same day.
5/9

No real path from learning to a job

Internshala owns both a training business and a job board, and completing a training changes nothing about your standing in their own funnel.

Hiero's answer: the connection is the product, not a cross-sell.
3/9

Candidates ghosted after being assessed

Pass every HackerRank test case and hear nothing for months. Apply on Internshala into silence. The assessment is a one-way mirror.

Hiero's answer: an honest outcome every time, even when automated.
3/9

Freshers structurally underserved

Remasto has no fresher track, LinkedIn ranks by a network they don't have, interviewing.io serves senior engineers chasing FAANG.

Hiero's answer: fresher track as a first-class path, already shipped.
3/9

Priced out of the Indian market

₹14,000/yr, $29/mo, ₹28,000 per session. Udemy is the exception at ₹400–700 — and the reason price cannot be our wedge.

Hiero's answer: INR-native pricing on outcomes, never per rep.

The honest summary

Hiero already closes four of the five repeating market gaps — gap analysis against a real JD, feedback that responds to your work, a loop that teaches and re-tests, and a path from learning into opportunities. It does so with proctoring that the nearest AI competitor lacks entirely. The one gap it does not yet close is the biggest one: 8 of 9 platforms produce no verifiable proof of competence, and neither does Hiero.

Where to go next

Where Hiero needs to improve

Ordered by how much market gap each one closes
1

Ship a verifiable, shareable record of competence

Points, tiers and scores exist but stay inside the product. Nothing leaves Hiero that an employer can open, check and trust. This is the single largest hole in the entire market — eight of nine platforms fail it, and LinkedIn is publicly retiring its attempt because hirers want evidence of applied skill.

Hiero already generates the raw material: proctored sessions, graded answers, evaluated solutions. The missing piece is turning that into a credential with a public verification link.

2

Build the employer side that consumes the proof

A credential with no one reading it is another participation certificate. The opportunities board tracks applications, but there is no recruiter-facing product that treats a Hiero record as a hiring signal.

Internshala's failure is the warning: they own learning and hiring and still never connected them.

3

Put the record where recruiters already look

LinkedIn is the default identity layer for work and is vacating skill verification. The verified Hiero record should sit on the LinkedIn profile rather than waiting to be visited.

Posture: "Hiero proves what LinkedIn can only claim."

4

Open the post-rejection channel

Millions of freshers fail HackerRank screens and Unstop competitions into total silence every year. "Here is what that test measured, here is where you actually failed, here is the fix" reaches people at maximum motivation. This is an acquisition channel, not a feature.

5

Consolidate the codebase and mark content freshness

The repository carries several parallel copies of the served app, so fixes must be applied repeatedly and risk landing in only one. Dozens of test pages sit at the project root. Separately, no track shows a "last verified" date — the staleness failure seen in both Coursera and Udemy.

Operational, not strategic — but it taxes every change on this list.
